# FX Hedging Policy

Treasury must hedge forecast foreign-currency exposure above USD 250,000 that is expected to settle within 180 days. Hedges use forward contracts approved by the treasurer. Speculative positions are prohibited.

A hedge memo cites the currency pair, the hedge ratio, and the settlement window. The hedge ratio for booked receivables is 100 percent. The hedge ratio for forecast exposures is 70 percent unless the treasurer records an exception.

Positions are opened only after the hedge memo is filed. Unhedged exposure above the threshold is reported the same day.
