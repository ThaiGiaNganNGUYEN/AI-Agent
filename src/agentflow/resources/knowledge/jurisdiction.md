# Jurisdiction Screening

Before paying a new beneficiary, screen the country and the counterparty. Sanctioned regions are prohibited. The reviewer records the country code, the local currency, and the decision.

Germany (DE) uses the euro and is an approved payment jurisdiction. The United States (US) is an approved payment jurisdiction. A new jurisdiction needs treasurer approval before the first payment.

Jurisdiction notes belong in the treasury memo next to any live FX rate. Do not treat a country profile as a sanctions clearance by itself.
