# Treasury Approval Matrix

Transactions under USD 50,000 require an operator. Transactions above USD 50,000 require an admin co-approval. Policy exceptions expire after 30 days and are written to the audit log.

GDP or other macro figures can inform a memo, but they do not replace the approval matrix. An economic indicator review still needs the usual approver when money moves.

The read-only role can view runs and policy. It cannot start a workflow.
